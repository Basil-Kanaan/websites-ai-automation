02What AI really is

AI isn't magic.

People can take on almost any kind of work.

AI takes on one narrow kind, faster than anyone.

Outside it, AI guesses. It isn't perfect either.

So what do you give it, and what do you keep?

Give it to AI

Narrow, repetitive, well-defined work

  • Answering the same questions, any hour
  • Sorting messages and pulling out the details
  • Following up on time, every time
  • Drafting replies and quotes for you to approve
  • Reminders, confirmations and review requests

Keep it with a person

Anything that needs context, judgment or facts it wasn't given

  • Anything it doesn't have the facts for. It will guess.
  • Judgment calls with real money on the line
  • Situations nobody planned for
  • Knowing your customers the way you do
  • Responsibility when something goes wrong

So I build systems that turn one person into an army.

The best of both worlds. AI does the repetitive work, and you and your team guide it, make the decisions and stay in control.
